# Electricity and Magnetism

<img src="https://clark.physics.illinois.edu/EMBanner.png" alt="EMBanner" >


Many real-world problems in electromagnetism are too complex for pencil-and-paper solutions. Computational methods offer a vital toolkit for tackling these challenges. These computational modules will introduce you to numerical techniques for solving problems in electrostatics and electrodynamics, enhancing both your understanding of the underlying physics and your ability to apply it in practical contexts.
